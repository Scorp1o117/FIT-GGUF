# M16 Granite Cross-Model Reveal — Preregistration + Protocol Lock

Date: 2026-08-29
Status: PREREGISTERED AND LOCKED — committed before any Granite quality
evaluation. The gates, ROPE, slice hashes, seeds, and provenance below are
frozen; results are recorded as-is under D-0021 (a non-transfer is a recorded
failure, not a tuning prompt).

## Purpose

Test whether the FIT package frozen in D-0021 transfers to a second, smaller,
architecturally different model: exact-size continuous control between
presets, and the allocator ordering (original utility vs block-balanced v0.1b
vs matched random) at FIT-50.

## Preregistered gates (delta δ = 1% relative macro-KL ROPE)

- **G-size (product claim):** every artifact's actual size equals its exact
  predicted size (zero-byte error) and every tensor's qtype matches the
  dry-run assignment. Failure = the core FIT product claim does not transfer.
- **G-util (utility transfer):** O50 macro KL beats the three-seed random
  mean by ≥ 1%, and O50 beats at least 2 of 3 seeds; any winning seed within
  the 1% ROPE. Failure = the imatrix utility does not transfer to Granite.
- **G-bal (v0.1b transfer):** B50 macro KL beats O50 by ≥ 1%. Failure = the
  block-balanced advantage does not transfer (v0.1b stays Huihui-specific).
- Guardrail: no artifact worse than the worse of O50/B50 by more than 25% in
  any domain.
- FIT-25/75 diagnostics and per-domain tables are reported and gate nothing.

## Decision rule (frozen)

- Results are recorded as-is. Transfer verdicts are stated per gate.
- If G-size fails, the milestone is a toolchain failure and blocks everything
  until root-caused.
- If G-util or G-bal fail, D-0021 records the non-transfer; no Granite-side
  tuning is permitted, and any future v0.2 development on Granite requires a
  third model for validation.
- No further original-model (Huihui) experiments are authorized in M16.
